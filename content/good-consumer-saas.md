I use [Kagi](https://kagi.com/) as my search engine This is a little piece of SaaS that my (relatively cheap) self pays for every month. It's probably the lowest RoI software that I could subscribe to as Google does almost everything that it does (apart from an excellent translation engine). The main reason that I use it is that it doesn't annoy me or change in any meaningful way. It stays out of my way. I search, it gives alright results and the job is done. I don't buy into any of their moral philosophiing around "protecting the web". It's just high quality software that does what it says on the tin without expecting or trying to be more. It holds a neutral stance.

Consumer SaaS have been taking quite a preditory stance in recent days. Some of this is due to post-ZIRP conditions. Many companies are in the position in which investor(s) have put a bunch of money into your venture when the weather was good and investments in tech were a safer bet. Lots of money comes into your hand with loose promises of returns. Post ZIRP the weather is not as favourable to you as you face headwinds. Consumer spending slumps, markets are frozen, revenue is down and suddenly your company isn't looking as attractive compared to just sticking it some bond. You need RoI for your investors as they are getting itchy feet. You start taking money from the "dark pile". Change the monitization while adding nothing. Implement dark patterns. Annoy the user. Did you want to upgrade to Pro+? It costs costs your reputation, demoralization for everyone and churn. You get what you want and flash bright for a second and remain dimmer afterwards. A quick hit. Perhaps we can stay afloat another quarter.

Software is a tool. A tool that changes every day creates a low trust relationship. A tool that whispers into my ear to use it more feels more like a bad drug dealer. People have lamented social media for this reason. I like to think that you cannot cheat the fundamentals. They will see straight through you. They will feel your company floundering for cash as you implement a paywall or move things around for higher engagement or whatever. Have you tried just being useful?
