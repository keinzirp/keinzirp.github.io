The largest gripe I have with IPhones is that IOS refuses to treat web browsers as first class citizens.
I know that this is by design and that they are doing this for that sweet sweet app store revenue.
The way this surfaces for me is that you can't just listen to audio sources from the web anymore with ease.

Have a podcast on a website that you want to listen to? It may or may not pause when you lock your phone and be wiped from your active listening state in the lockscreen UI. Oh you hit the roll of the dice correctly and it did play? Don't ever pause because it's going to start a random apple podcast when you start playing again.

I get the forces at play but it really feels like we are scraping the bottom of the barrel of how hostile we can make an operating system for users. I clearly have an intent. Just let me use my phone for that.
