Many thanks to what I'm calling the pilfer bin at the current hostel that I'm staying at.
This accomodation is perfect. It has a high throughput of people which are travellers in a relatively well off part of the world.
This means that people are willing to spend it up on supermarket goods that they probably are not going consuming all of. When they don't eat all of it it goes into the free food bin.

I have a joke of receiving a full english breakfast from the pilfer bin. Today wasn't actually that far off, (eggs, milk, bread, beans, apples).
It's always full when you're waking up the earliest in the hostel. I basically haven't spent any money on breakfasts and snacks.
Over my three week stay here I have gotten:
- Bread
- Eggs
- Milk
- Muesli
- Apples, bananas, other fruit
- Yogurt
- Cheese
- Cucumbers, carrots
- All types of legumes
- Random sweets

![pilfer](/images/pilfer.jpeg)

I'm always reminded of the essay ["How to Drop Out"](https://ranprieur.com/essays/dropout.html) when I'm eating something from here because of his line of dumpster diving for eggs. I know this is nothing in comparision but I bet that guy would dream of a system like this.
