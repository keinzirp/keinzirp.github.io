I've been discussing branding recently and have come across a laundry list of patterns which I find strange. [Notion](https://www.notion.com/) is a good example of a strong brand that are making some moves which highlight a trend.

The landing page says "think together". This is a bit of a diversion from their fun little mascot. They are now "serious".

![notion](/images/notion.png)

I don't really know what Notion as a product does at this point and I feel like I'm being sold to. I find these sorts of slogans come out of marketing teams which have taken "10 steps back" to try capture the scope of a product. When your product does a lot you may end up in a place like this where you have lost a lot of resolution. What does Notion do again? Moving on to the their centre element.

![notion-space](/images/notion-space.png)

The video starts and you're drawing parallels to the Apollo 11 moon landing. I think we should remind ourselves that this is one of mankinds greatest achievements. You cannot seriously associate yourself anywhere near this sort of achievement without coming across as a bit of a con. In a product you're allowed to be bold and say cool stuff but it has to be at least grounded in some sort of reality. Statements about your product are more concrete than assocations.

I've just spent 5 minutes on your website. What does Notion do again?
